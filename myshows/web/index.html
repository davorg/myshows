    HOW TO READ THIS DASHBOARD

    Status headlines are dated, sourced editorial updates. Official announcements and attributable reporting are labelled separately. Renewal, production, release and ending facts are independent: a cancelled show can still have an unreleased final season.

    Announced windows keep their original precision. No month or day is invented. A passed release announcement is marked for review rather than automatically turned into “now streaming”. Status evidence checked more than 30 days ago is flagged for another check.

    Progress comes from Trakt and covers aired regular episodes, including hidden seasons and excluding specials. “Most recently watched” follows viewing timestamps; “furthest watched” follows episode order. Earlier gaps remain unwatched. Rewatch-reset limitations are shown in the details.

    This is a saved snapshot. Opening the page does not refresh Trakt or check news sources. Watched data more than 24 hours old is labelled separately from status evidence. All progress times use your browser's timezone; announcement dates retain their stated region.
